## 🚀 Business Scenario
The company was facing an unexpected increase in turnover. The mission was to build an interactive solution to answer:
- **Who is leaving?** (High-Risk Departments).
- **Is pay enough?** (Salary vs. Tenure correlation).
- **Who is responsible?** (Manager-level attrition analysis).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Power BI:** Data modeling & DAX.
- **Power Query:** Advanced data cleaning & transformation.
- **Figma:** Custom UI/UX Design & Layout.
- **DAX Measures:** Calculated Attrition %, Tenure, and High-Risk identifying logic.

## 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations
- **Department Alert:** The **Production** department shows the highest risk level.
- **Leadership Gap:** Significant attrition noticed under specific Manager IDs, indicating a need for leadership training.
- **Retention Strategy:** Data shows that high salaries don't always equate to longer tenure; workplace culture and management play a vital role.
